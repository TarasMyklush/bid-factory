Status as of 29 October 2026. Figures are the best available; several are dated and marked so.

Working estimate: roughly 100,000 registered public-sector suppliers across the GCC, of which 10,000 to 15,000 bid often enough to need help but cannot justify a bid team. At AED 30,000 to 60,000 of outsourced bid spend per year each, the serviceable market is AED 300 to 900 million a year. A 1 to 2% success fee on Saudi SME awards alone would be a theoretical pool of SAR 220 to 440 million. Obtainable for a small firm within three years: AED 5 to 15 million a year in fees. The prime-bidding endgame is not capped by fees.

## Competition

### Global RFP and proposal software

Loopio and Responsive sell enterprise seats on request. Arphie, Tribble, Inventive and AutogenAI are the AI-native generation; AutoRFP starts at about $899 a month. All are English-first, assume the client runs a proposal team, and know nothing of Etimad, Tejari, local content, ICV or Saudisation scoring. They are tools, not a done-for-you service.

### Gulf agencies

Labeeb in Dubai publishes prices from AED 2,500 per bid in the UAE and SAR 615 for a single proposal in Saudi, fixed fee, remote, no win guarantee, no success fee. Other providers are business-plan writers and PR firms that take tenders occasionally. No Gulf provider found offers a compliance matrix, a gap report, or measured outcomes.

### In-house bid managers

Present at larger suppliers, which marks the ceiling of the target segment.

### The client's own ChatGPT

Produces a generic narrative. Does not produce a clause-complete matrix, a gap report with deadlines, or a portal-ready pack, and invents claims. That is the line the service sells across.

### Big consultancies

Serve mega-bids. Not the segment.
